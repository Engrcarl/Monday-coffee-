# Monday-coffee-

## Project Objective
The aim of this project is to analyze the online sales data of Monday Coffee, which has been selling its products since January 2023. Based on consumer demand and sales performance, the goal is to recommend the top three cities in India that would be ideal for opening new coffee shop locations.

### Key Analysis Areas & Questions
1. Coffee Consumer Base in Each City
Since 25% of a city’s population consumes coffee, how many potential coffee drinkers exist in each location?

2. Total Coffee Sales Revenue
What was the total revenue generated from coffee sales across all cities in the last quarter of 2023?

3. Product Sales Volume
How many units of each coffee product have been sold across different cities?

4. Average Sales Per Customer by City
What is the average amount spent per customer in each city?

5. City-Wise Population & Coffee Consumers
Provide a list of cities, their total population, and the estimated number of coffee consumers.

6. Best-Selling Coffee Products in Each City
Which top three coffee products are the best sellers in each city based on sales volume?

7. Customer Base by City
How many unique customers have purchased coffee products in each city?

8. Sales vs Rent Comparison
For each city, compare the average sales per customer with the average rent per customer to assess financial feasibility.

9. Monthly Sales Growth Trend
What is the percentage growth (or decline) in coffee sales over different months?

10. Identifying the Best Cities for Expansion
Which three cities have the highest sales performance, considering key factors such as:

*Total Sales Revenue

*Total Rent Costs

*Total Number of Customers

*Estimated Coffee Consumers

By evaluating these metrics, we can identify the top three cities that offer the best market potential for Monday Coffee to expand its physical store presence.
